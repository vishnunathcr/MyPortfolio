Fieldnote

Brand identity, 2025

A visual identity for a seed library that lends packets of heirloom seeds. The system grows from one cut-paper shape that changes with the season.

Role
Lead designer
Scope
Logo, packaging, signage
Year
2025
Result
Loans up 3x in a year

The challenge

The library had no brand and a tiny budget. It needed something members could recognise on a packet, a sign and a tote bag without a style guide.

The approach

I cut a single leaf shape from paper and photographed it in four seasonal colours. That shape became the logo, the packet pattern and the shelf signage.

The outcome

Loans tripled in the first year and volunteers began making their own packets in the same style.
