Overview

A bookkeeping app for freelancers who hate bookkeeping. I redesigned invoicing so a first invoice takes under a minute.

Role
Product designer
Scope
Research, UI, design system
Team
Product designer, 2 engineers, 1 product manager
Duration
4 months
Platform
iOS, Android, web
Tools
Figma, FigJam, Maze

The problem

New users abandoned setup before sending an invoice. Interviews showed they were afraid of getting tax details wrong.

Goals

  • Cut onboarding drop-off
  • Let users send a first invoice in under a minute
  • Keep tax setup trustworthy

Success metrics

  • Onboarding completion rate
  • Median time to first invoice
  • Support tickets about tax setup

Research

Methods

  • 14 interviews with freelancers
  • Analysis of onboarding funnel data
  • Usability tests of the old flow with 6 users

Key insights

01

Tax fear stalls setup

Users left at the tax form because they worried about getting it wrong.

02

Invoicing is the real goal

People signed up to get paid, not to do bookkeeping.

03

Phones start the first invoice

Most first invoices began on mobile between meetings.

Who it is for

Arjun, 29

Freelance illustrator
“I just want to get paid without learning accounting.”

Needs

  • Send an invoice fast
  • Know the numbers are correct

Frustrations

  • Long forms before any value
  • Jargon like accrual basis

Defining the opportunity

How might we let freelancers send an invoice first and handle tax details when they matter?

Design

I moved tax setup after the first invoice, replaced forms with plain-language questions, and built a small component library to keep the screens consistent.

Key decisions

  1. Invoice first, tax later

    Moved tax setup after the first invoice so users reach value in minutes.

  2. Plain-language questions

    Replaced accounting terms with questions like “Do you charge sales tax?”.

  3. A small component library

    Reusable inputs and cards kept web and mobile consistent.

Key visuals

Testing and iteration

  • Round 1: tested a prototype with 8 freelancers in Maze. Tax questions still caused hesitation.
  • Round 2: added inline examples and a skip option. Completion improved and hesitation dropped.

Results

Onboarding drop-off halved and the median time to first invoice fell below one minute.

50%Lower onboarding drop-off
<1 minMedian time to first invoice
-30%Tax setup support tickets

Reflection

Ordering the flow around the user's goal mattered more than any visual change. Next: recurring invoices and payment reminders.
